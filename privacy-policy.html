Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: Habit does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

This Privacy Policy explains how Habit – Build Better Days (“Habit”, “the app”), published by Muhammad Ibrahim (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the app on Android or iOS.

1. Information you enter

You can add the following to the app:

  • Habits you create: name, icon, colour, category, the days they repeat on and an optional note.
  • The days you mark each habit as done.
  • Optional daily journal notes.
  • Your appearance settings (light/dark theme and accent colour).

This information is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate. We do not have access to it.

2. Information we do not collect

Habit does not:

  • require an account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number;
  • collect your location, contacts, photos, files or device identifiers;
  • include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools;
  • sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

3. Internet access

The app has internet permission for a single purpose: on first launch it downloads its typeface (the “Inter” font) from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google's servers receive your device's IP address and basic request information to deliver the font. No habit data, notes or personal information is included. Google's handling of this request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. After the font is downloaded the app works fully offline.

4. Exporting to Excel

When you choose Export Excel, the app creates a spreadsheet of your habits on your device and opens your system's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send or save it (for example Files, Google Drive, email or a messaging app). That destination's own privacy policy then applies. The app does not upload the file anywhere by itself.

5. Device backups

If you have device backups switched on (Android Backup or iCloud Backup), your phone's operating system may include the app's data in those backups. This is controlled by your device settings and by Google or Apple, not by us.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual habits inside the app, or delete everything at once by uninstalling Habit or clearing its storage in your device settings. Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

7. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Please protect your device with a screen lock to keep your information private.

8. Children's privacy

Habit is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

10. Contact
